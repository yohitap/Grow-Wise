# 🌿 GrowWise — AI-Powered Gardening Platform

**Grow smarter. Grow greener. Grow anywhere.**

GrowWise is an AI-powered gardening platform designed to make home gardening simpler, smarter, and more accessible. Whether someone is growing vegetables on a terrace, herbs on a balcony, or indoor ornamental plants, GrowWise provides practical guidance to help them grow healthier plants with confidence.

By combining plant knowledge, personalized recommendations, organic gardening practices, and AI-assisted support, GrowWise bridges the gap between beginner gardeners and successful home gardening.

## 🌱 Why GrowWise?

Many people want to grow their own plants but struggle with questions like: *Which plants are suitable for my space? How often should I water them? Why are their leaves turning yellow? How can I control pests without relying on harsh chemicals?*

Gardening information is often scattered across websites, videos, and online communities. Beginners may find conflicting advice or struggle to identify the right solution for their specific plants and growing conditions.

**GrowWise brings essential gardening resources together in one accessible platform**, helping users make informed decisions, prevent common plant-care mistakes, and adopt more sustainable gardening habits.

## ✨ Key Highlights

- **🌿 Comprehensive Plant Encyclopedia:** Explore plants with detailed information on sunlight, watering, soil, container requirements, growth duration, harvesting, and common plant problems.
- **🤖 AI Gardening Assistant:** Get conversational guidance for plant care, gardening problems, organic fertilizers, pests, diseases, and seasonal growing advice.
- **🔍 AI Plant Identification:** Upload a plant photo to support plant identification and discover relevant care recommendations.
- **🩺 Plant Disease Assistance:** Analyze images of unhealthy plants to explore possible diseases, their causes, and potential treatments.
- **🪴 Personalized Garden Management:** Organize favorite plants, track garden progress, and manage watering reminders through a dedicated dashboard.
- **♻️ Organic Fertilizer Library:** Discover homemade fertilizer methods using materials such as banana peels, eggshells, rice water, compost, and neem-based solutions.
- **🐛 Organic Pest & Disease Management:** Learn about common plant problems, their symptoms, prevention techniques, and possible organic treatments.
- **📅 Seasonal Gardening Calendar:** Explore planting suggestions for different months and growing seasons.
- **🌤️ Smart Plant Recommendations:** Find plants suited to available space, sunlight, climate, and gardening experience.

## 💡 What Makes GrowWise Different?

GrowWise focuses on **practical, beginner-friendly, and sustainable gardening**, rather than simply providing a catalogue of plants.

- **One gardening companion:** Plant information, care guidance, fertilizer resources, pest management, and garden organization are brought together in one platform.
- **Space-aware gardening:** Supports terrace, balcony, and indoor gardeners instead of focusing exclusively on large gardens or farms.
- **Actionable guidance:** Emphasizes clear care instructions, prevention strategies, and practical next steps that users can apply at home.
- **Sustainability by design:** Encourages organic gardening methods, responsible use of household resources, and waste-to-compost practices.
- **AI-assisted problem solving:** Aims to make gardening advice more accessible by helping users understand plant problems and explore relevant solutions.

## 🪴 How to Use GrowWise

1. **Explore plants:** Open the Plant Encyclopedia, search for a plant, and filter by category.
2. **Learn plant care:** Open a plant's details to review its growing requirements and maintenance guidance.
3. **Get gardening help:** Use the AI Gardening Assistant to ask questions about plant care and common gardening problems.
4. **Investigate plant issues:** Use the plant identification or disease assistance interface when image-analysis functionality is available.
5. **Choose sustainable methods:** Browse homemade fertilizer recipes and organic pest-management resources.
6. **Plan your garden:** Explore seasonal planting suggestions and plant recommendations.
7. **Manage your plants:** Use the dashboard to organize favorites, monitor progress, and manage reminders where supported.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) and ensure MongoDB is available locally or through MongoDB Atlas if your backend requires it.

### Installation

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/yohitap/Grow-Wise.git
cd Grow-Wise
```

Install backend dependencies:

```bash
cd backend
npm install
```

Configure the required environment variables using your project's environment configuration, then start the backend using the script defined in `backend/package.json`.

Open `index.html` in a browser or use a local development server to explore the frontend. Some features may require the backend to be running.

> **Note:** Database credentials and API keys should be stored in environment variables, never committed to GitHub. Follow the actual configuration and scripts in the repository when setting up the application.
